Privacy policy: Upgrade Log Explainer

Effective 26 November 2026

Upgrade Log Explainer is a Windows app made by Haitham Eldesouky. This page explains what the app does with information on your PC. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing.

What the app reads

The app reads Windows log files so it can explain them: setup logs (such as setupact.log, setuperr.log and CompatData files in the Panther folders), CBS.log, dism.log and SetupDiagResults.log. It reads them only from the default Windows log folders when you click "Scan this PC", or from files and folders you choose yourself. These logs can contain your computer name, user name, and lists of installed apps and drivers.

What the app does with it

  • Everything is processed on your PC, in memory, while the app is open.
  • The app has no internet features. It does not upload, share or sell any information.
  • It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It does not store the logs or the results. A report is only written to disk when you click "Save report" and choose where to save it.
  • It does not change any system settings or files.

Microsoft Store

The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect information about installs, purchases and app crashes under its own terms; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer only sees the summary reports Microsoft provides in Partner Center, which do not identify you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
