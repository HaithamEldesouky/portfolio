FROGGER

Hop. Dodge. Float. Get home.

How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, swipe or use the buttons.
  • Roads: cross without touching a vehicle.
  • River: hop onto logs and turtles. Water is not your friend.
  • Goal: fill all five homes on the top row. Catch a fly for a big bonus.
  • Watch out: the timer, and turtles that dive from level 2.
  • Pause: P or Esc.
